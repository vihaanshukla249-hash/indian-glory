01 / THE BEGINNING
A CIVILISATION THAT NEVER STOPPED

INDIA WAS NEVER JUST A COUNTRY.

It was a centre of ideas, trade, science, philosophy, art and human civilisation.

Across thousands of years, Indian civilisation developed cities, universities, mathematical concepts, languages, literature, architecture and traditions that continue to influence the world today.
