A CIVILISATION IN STONE

BUILT BY
GENERATIONS.

Indian architecture evolved through thousands of years. Harappan cities demonstrated sophisticated urban planning, Buddhist caves preserved extraordinary rock-cut art, Hindu and Jain temples transformed stone into sculpture, while Islamic and Mughal builders introduced new forms, geometry and monumental scale.

02 • EXPLORE

ICONS OF INDIA

Explore India's architectural legacy across different periods, traditions and regions.

ANCIENT • GUJARAT

Dholavira

One of the remarkable cities of the Indus Valley Civilisation, known for sophisticated water management and planned urban design.

TEMPLE • ODISHA

Konark Sun Temple

A monumental 13th-century temple designed as a gigantic stone chariot dedicated to Surya, the Sun God.

FORT • DELHI

Red Fort

Built by Shah Jahan in the 17th century, the Red Fort became one of the most important symbols of Mughal power in Delhi.

MUGHAL • AGRA

Taj Mahal

Commissioned by Shah Jahan, the Taj Mahal is renowned for its white marble, symmetry, gardens and intricate decorative craftsmanship.

FORT • RAJASTHAN

Mehrangarh Fort

Rising above Jodhpur, this massive fort contains palaces, courtyards and finely decorated interiors built over centuries.

TEMPLE • TAMIL NADU

Brihadisvara Temple

Built under the Chola dynasty, this monumental temple is celebrated for its towering vimana and remarkable stone engineering.

CAVES • MAHARASHTRA

Ajanta Caves

Buddhist rock-cut caves famous for their murals, sculptures and extraordinary artistic tradition.

COLONIAL • KOLKATA

Victoria Memorial

A monumental marble building combining British architectural ideas with influences from Indian traditions.
