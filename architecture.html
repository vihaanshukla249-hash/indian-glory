A CIVILISATION BUILT IN STONE

India built
more than monuments.

It built ideas that survived centuries.

Indian architecture reflects thousands of years of experimentation in engineering, mathematics, religion, art and urban planning. Every region developed its own visual language while remaining connected to the larger story of the civilisation.

MASTERPIECE

The Taj Mahal

A monument of Mughal architecture and one of the world's most recognised expressions of symmetry, craftsmanship and monumental design.

AGRA · UTTAR PRADESH

Taj Mahal

Commissioned by Shah Jahan in memory of Mumtaz Mahal, the Taj Mahal was constructed in the 17th century. Its white marble structure, geometric planning, domes, arches and intricate decorative work represent the extraordinary refinement of Mughal architecture.
