INDIA'S JOURNEY

A NATION REMEMBERS

Freedom was shaped by millions of voices.

India's struggle for independence was not a single movement or the work of one person. It developed through many campaigns, organisations and forms of resistance.

Some led mass movements. Others worked underground, organised communities, spread ideas or challenged colonial rule in their own regions.

THE JOURNEY

Resistance to Freedom

Important moments in India's struggle against British colonial rule.

1857

Great Uprising

A major uprising against British rule spread across parts of northern and central India.

1905

Swadeshi Movement

The partition of Bengal triggered a powerful movement promoting Swadeshi, national education and self-reliance.

1919

A New Phase

Mass politics expanded and the national movement entered a new period of widespread participation.

1930

Civil Disobedience

Gandhi's Salt March became a defining moment in the Civil Disobedience Movement.

1942

Quit India

The Quit India Movement demanded an immediate end to British rule.

1947

Independence

India became independent on 15 August 1947.
