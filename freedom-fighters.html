THE JOURNEY

Resistance to Freedom

Key moments across India's independence movement.

1857
THE GREAT UPRISING

A major challenge to British rule

Soldiers, rulers, peasants and communities across several regions rose against colonial authority.

1905
SWADESHI

A movement for self-reliance

The Swadeshi movement encouraged indigenous goods, national education and resistance to colonial policies.

1919
MASS POLITICS

The movement reaches millions

National politics increasingly became a mass movement involving people across India.

1930
CIVIL DISOBEDIENCE

The Salt March

The Salt March became one of the most recognisable acts of civil disobedience against colonial rule.

1942
QUIT INDIA

A nationwide demand for freedom

The Quit India Movement demanded an immediate end to British rule.

1947
INDEPENDENCE

India becomes independent

On 15 August 1947, India became an independent nation.
