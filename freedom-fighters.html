FEATURED STORY

A SYMBOL OF COURAGE

The story of India's freedom belongs to millions.

From the uprising of 1857 to the final years before independence, countless people contributed to the freedom movement in different ways.

Some led mass movements. Some organized resistance. Some worked underground. Others spread ideas, built organizations, supported movements or inspired entire generations.

THE JOURNEY

From Resistance to Freedom

Explore important periods in India's struggle against colonial rule.

1857

The Great Uprising

A major uprising against British rule involving soldiers, rulers, peasants and communities across several regions.

1905

Swadeshi Movement

The movement encouraged self-reliance, indigenous goods and opposition to colonial policies.

1919

A New Phase

The national movement entered a new phase of mass political participation and resistance.

1930

Civil Disobedience

The Salt March and wider civil disobedience campaigns became important moments in the independence movement.

1942

Quit India Movement

A mass movement demanding an end to British rule spread across India.

1947

Independence

India became independent on 15 August 1947.
